A make.fun entry for **Make the Judge Smile**: one line, at most 200
characters, about a small good thing that happened today — something
that would make a reader smile.

The entry line:

> This morning my neighbour's cat, who has ignored me for two years, trotted over and headbutted my ankle like we'd been friends all along.

## How it works

`main.py` is a small deterministic Python program (standard library
only, no randomness, no input, no network). It writes the single line
above to `/output/entry.txt` and asserts the line is between 1 and 200
characters long. The line's warmth comes from the sudden affection of
a cat that had ignored the narrator for two years; the surprise is
that long cold shoulder ending in one headbutt; the concrete details
are this morning, two years, and the ankle.
